# ProofLabAI Founder Blueprint

This document contains complete suggestions for evolving ProofLabAI into a focused startup. Written as a reference guide in simple English.

## 1. One Core Mission

Turn resume claims into trusted proof and guide every candidate from resume to placement.

Every feature should answer one question: Does this help the candidate prove their skills or become interview ready? If not, move it to a later version.

## 2. Product Philosophy

- Do not become another resume builder.
- Do not become another coding platform.
- Do not become another job portal.
- Become the AI Career Operating System that understands, verifies and guides each candidate.

## 3. Complete User Journey

1. Signup and create profile.
2. Upload resume.
3. Optionally connect GitHub, LinkedIn and coding profiles.
4. Select target role and companies.
5. AI extracts skills, projects and certifications.
6. Candidate confirms extracted information.
7. AI creates a personalized assessment.
8. Candidate completes MCQs, coding, debugging and project questions.
9. Proof Engine analyzes strengths and weaknesses.
10. AI creates Proof Scores.
11. Candidate chooses career goal.
12. AI generates personalized roadmap.
13. Daily missions begin.
14. Learning + practice + mini projects.
15. Project verification.
16. Weekly progress report.
17. Mock interviews.
18. Company readiness analysis.
19. Job recommendations.
20. Placement tracking.
21. Career growth after placement.

## 4. The Proof Engine

Everything should revolve around a single engine:

Resume Claim → Verification → Evidence Collection → Proof Score → Roadmap → Retest → Verified Proof Profile

## 5. AI Guidance After Assessment

- Show AI diagnosis instead of only marks.
- Ask the candidate to choose a career goal.
- Create a personalized learning path.
- Generate one daily mission instead of many tasks.
- Teach weak concepts before testing again.
- Recommend mini projects based on weak skills.
- Review uploaded projects and GitHub repositories.
- Generate weekly progress reports.
- Unlock mock interviews after enough skills are verified.
- Recommend jobs only after verification.

## 6. Features That Keep Users Returning

- Daily missions
- Learning streaks
- Weekly reports
- Proof level upgrades
- Career timeline
- Skill re-verification
- Personal AI coach
- Personalized reminders
- Company readiness score
- Project building challenges

## 7. New Ideas Worth Building

**Knowledge Graph** — Track every sub-topic inside a skill.

**Skill Passport** — A living profile containing verified skills, projects and growth.

**Proof Wallet** — Share verified skills with recruiters.

**AI Memory** — Remember previous assessments and adapt future questions.

**Adaptive Learning** — Teach, practice, retest and verify.

**Command Center** — One home screen showing today's mission, roadmap and progress.

**Career OS** — Everything revolves around one evolving AI profile rather than many disconnected modules.

## 8. Suggested Versions

**Version 1** — Resume upload, Proof Engine, assessment, roadmap, dashboard, retest and Proof Profile.

**Version 2** — Voice reasoning, AI interviewer, adaptive learning, advanced project verification.

**Version 3** — Recruiter dashboard, college dashboard, opportunity tracking, certification radar and mentor review.

## 9. Principles While Building

- Keep every screen simple.
- One clear next action for the user.
- Do not overwhelm with too many features.
- Always explain why a score was given.
- Show progress instead of only marks.
- Focus on helping, not judging.
- Think long-term career guidance, not one-time testing.

## 10. Final Vision

ProofLabAI should feel like an AI mentor that stays with a candidate from the first resume upload until their first job and beyond. Assessment is only the beginning. The real product is continuous guidance, verification, improvement and trusted proof.
