## 3. What ProofLab is

A proof-of-skill platform for Indian engineering colleges.
1. A college uploads a student CSV.
2. Each student gets one real piece of work a day (a **Lot**), does it inside the app, and explains it out loud for 60 seconds.
3. Students learn on **Tracks** (17 tracks, 167 topics, lessons + quizzes).
4. They compete in **squads** of about 11 inside their section.
5. Companies see proof profiles.

Dashboards (4):
- **Student**
- **College (TPO)**
- **Admin**
- **Company**: Startup and Recruiter merged into one role; the database role is still `startup`.

Live site: **https://prooflab.co.in**

## 6. How the product works now

**Student flow**
1. First login, `/student/start`: upload a resume (or skip).
2. The resume is scored: **quality** and **ATS match**.
   - Under 60% ATS: a **one-time Auto-fix with AI**. One call rewrites the resume, re-scores it, and gives next steps plus a motivation line. It is stored, so asking again costs nothing.
   - Download as PDF.
3. **Resume test.** 11 questions: 30 s per choice question, 90 s per written one, Next button pinned at the bottom. Then a coding round of 2 problems in the editor.
4. Grading gives a scorecard and a roadmap.
   - The student is **placed on Tracks** from their chosen interests.
   - **Known gap:** a student with target role "Other" and no interests is not placed automatically. They can still pick a track themselves.
5. Roadmap mistakes appear as **"Revise"** hints on the Tracks ladder (every track) and in "Other things your test caught".
6. **Daily Lot**: one real task a day from the content library.
   - The task shows as **"Your task" in simple words**, with an "Show original wording" button.
   - Coding tasks open the editor, where the student runs and submits.
   - Other tasks are a **written answer** graded against a checklist.
   - **No upload proof anywhere.**
   - Record a 60-second voice explanation. It is transcribed with clickable timestamps.
7. **Tracks** (17 tracks, 167 topics, 1,325 lesson steps):
   - Each step shows as cards: the lesson, a code example, a **Go deeper** card (worked example, common mistakes, "try this"), and sometimes **Read more** cards from open-licence GitHub repos.
   - Each topic ends with a quiz. The questions were written once by AI; checking is direct, with no AI.

**The "simple question" system**
- `task-explain` function, `task_explainers` table, and `_shared/explain.ts`.
- The question is retold in child-simple words: one line, what it means, steps, a tiny example, words to know, and "you are done when".
- Stored once per distinct task text and shared by everyone on the same task.
- A new Lot gets its simple version the moment `lot-writer` writes it. Other tasks get theirs on first open (about 5 s, once).
- All 37 Lots and 34 tasks are already done.

**College**
- Four pages: Home, Students, Squads, Insights. They auto-refresh.
- Import a CSV → one email per student → **squads form automatically per section** (about 11 each).
- Remove students: tick boxes → "Remove selected", or 🗑 per row. A backup is written to `removed_students` first.

**Admin**
- Four sidebar items with tabs (`src/components/dashboard/admin/adminNav.ts`).
- Companies tab and hiring activity.
- **Content Library**: every crawled page, with Read and Hide/Show.
- Remove student.

**Company**
- `/company/dashboard`: Home · Talent · Work · Jobs.
- Old `/startup/dashboard` and `/recruiter/dashboard` redirect there.

**Three ways to remove a student**
1. College dashboard.
2. Admin dashboard.
3. Deleting the Google login in the console. The sync picks it up within 10 minutes.

All three go through `remove_students()`, which backs up first.
